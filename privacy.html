Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Personal Video Uploader is a private application used to automate video uploads to the owner's YouTube channel.

2. Information Accessed

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access the YouTube Data API with the account owner's authorization.

The application requests only the permissions necessary for its video upload functionality.

3. Use of Information

Google account authorization is used solely to upload videos and associated metadata to the owner's YouTube channel.

4. Data Storage

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally and are not publicly disclosed.

5. Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google user data with third parties. Video content and metadata are sent to YouTube to perform authorized uploads.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The application owner may delete locally stored credentials and revoke access through Google Account settings.

7. Security

Authentication credentials are kept private and are not included in publicly accessible source files.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries, please contact the application owner using the developer contact email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
